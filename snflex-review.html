SNFLEX 600W Macerating Toilet System Review: Add a Basement Bathroom Without Breaking Concrete

Adding a full or half bathroom in a basement, garage, attic, or guest room usually comes with a massive price tag. Traditional plumbing requires breaking through concrete slabs and digging trenches to reach the main sewer line.

The SNFLEX Two-Piece 600W Macerating Toilet System by SimpleProject is designed to solve that exact problem—letting you install a complete bathroom setup above floor level in a fraction of the time and cost.

Why Homeowners Choose SNFLEX for Basement Renovations

  • Saves Thousands on Underground Plumbing: Installs directly on top of any finished floor without jackhammering concrete or re-routing main drain lines.
  • Connects Your Entire Bathroom: Equipped with 1-1/2" side inlets so you can connect a sink, shower, bathtub, or even a washing machine to the same 600W pump.
  • Clean, Hidden Behind-the-Wall Option: Includes an extension pipe that allows you to conceal the macerator pump neatly behind a wall for a modern look.
  • Quiet & Odor-Free Operation: Features a built-in activated carbon filter to neutralize odors and operates quietly at 35–45 dBA.
  • Water-Saving Dual Flush: WaterSense-certified with 1.28 / 0.8 GPF flush options to reduce water usage without sacrificing flushing power.

Who Is This Upflush System Best For?

If you are finishing a basement, converting a garage into an in-law suite, or adding a bathroom below sewer level, this system provides an efficient all-in-one solution. With prepped water and discharge lines, installation can typically be completed in around 30 minutes.
